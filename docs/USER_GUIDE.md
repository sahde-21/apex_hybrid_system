# User Guide — SCF Enterprise Suite 1.0

## Getting started

1. Open the application URL provided by your administrator.
2. Sign in with your email and password.
3. Use the sidebar to open your modules (visibility depends on your role).
4. Switch language with the locale control (English, Arabic, Central Kurdish).

## Common tasks

### Sales

1. Create or select a customer (Contacts).
2. Create a quotation → convert to sales order when accepted.
3. Issue an invoice and record payment.
4. Print or export PDF from the document actions where available.

### Purchasing

1. Create a purchase request.
2. Convert to purchase order.
3. Receive goods into a warehouse.
4. Track supplier documents and totals.

### Inventory

1. Maintain products (SKU, prices, stock).
2. Adjust stock and transfer between warehouses when permitted.
3. Monitor low-stock indicators on dashboards/reports.

### Accounting

1. Record expenses and journal entries as authorized.
2. Review financial reports through Accounting and BI menus.
3. Do not reverse posted financial documents without following company policy.

### HR

1. Maintain employee records.
2. Record attendance and process leave requests according to workflow.
3. Run payroll generation when your role allows.

### CRM / Support / Projects

1. Convert leads according to your CRM process.
2. Manage support tickets through their lifecycle.
3. Update project tasks; use knowledge base articles when available.

### Dashboards and intelligence

1. Open the main dashboard for widgets available to your role.
2. Managers with intelligence access can open the Intelligence workspace for KPIs, alerts, and recommendations.

## Tips

- Validation messages appear on forms when required fields are missing.
- Unauthorized actions show a branded 403 page — contact your administrator for access.
- Export and print actions require export/report permissions.

For admin setup and recovery, see `ADMINISTRATOR_GUIDE.md`.
